## Original content

Two dimension-specific public camera profiles: autographics.runtime_camera2d/1 (103/14) and runtime_camera3d/1 (104/15), assembled v5+character+camera units; old behavior/1..5, character/1, character3d/1 assemblies byte-identical. Run-owned finite service: 2D follow center=clamp(actor, min+view/2, max-view/2) with equal-ratio letterbox (sample 570x380, 115px bars, bars take no game pointer); 3D orbit eye=target+d*(sin yaw cos pitch, sin pitch, cos yaw cos pitch) with distance [4,12] init 8, pitch [15,75] init 45, projection perspective fov60/near.05/far256 or ortho size [1,64], yaw normalized from 0. Host owns pointer/projection math: LMB drag 0.25 deg/logical px, wheel 1.1^(-notches) with the 856 40px/notch +-8 notch rule; fixed scalar accumulators with saturation caps and a diagnostic counter. One candidate folds per dispatch, sent via _ag_cam3_basis + _ag_cam3_orbit, echoed in the tick camera block and admitted bit-exactly (named rejections: configuration missing/domain, orbit initial/echo mismatch, configuration drift, run/tick). Fault codes 27/29/30. Camera reads are free (32-credit cap unchanged, proven by 32/32 contrast). Frame+camera pass prepare/accept/publish as one transaction; failure keeps the last-good pair; Stop restores the editing view; camera state never enters Document/dirty/Undo. Frozen contract: docs/plans/attachments/875/work/runtime-camera-contract.md.
